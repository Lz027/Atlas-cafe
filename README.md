# Atlas Cafe

Atlas Cafe

Concept:
A Substack‑style platform for coffee lovers: recipes, journals, history, and community submissions, with a future angle for small cafés to advertise.

Core pillars:

Recipes (50+ premade)

Each recipe includes:

Name, style (Espresso, V60, French Press, Cold Brew, etc.).

Coffee type (Arabica, Robusta, blend), origin, roast level.

Measurements (grams, water ratio, temperature, time).

Step‑by‑step instructions.

Users can:

Browse by method, strength, flavor notes.

“Try your own combination”:

Select coffee type, method, flavor preferences.

Site suggests a recipe or lets them tweak measurements.

Journal & History

Articles on:

Coffee history (origins, trade, culture).

Brewing science (extraction, grind size, ratios).

Regions & farms (with maps, short stories).

Each article has trusted citations (books, papers, reputable sites).

This becomes the “authority” layer.

Community Submissions

Users can:

Submit their own coffee recipes.

Write short “brew journals” (what they tried, what worked).

Input form includes:

Measurement fields (grams, ml, time, temp).

Dropdowns/tags for common words (e.g., “chocolatey”, “fruity”, “high acidity”).

For now, submissions can be stored in a JSON/CSV or simple backend (or even a Google Form → sheet) to keep it simple.

Future angle: small cafés

Later, you can add:

“Featured cafés” section.

Cafés can submit their signature recipes or stories.

Simple advertising/sponsorship model (e.g., “This recipe is brought to you by [Café X]”).



Coffee platform – recipes, journal, and community for coffee lovers.

Features guided recipe builder with measurement inputs and flavor tags.

Designed content structure and UI for recipes, articles, and community contributions.
